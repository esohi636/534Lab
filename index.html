Calculations and interpretation

October 2023 through October 2026 spans 36 elapsed months and 37 monthly price observations. Complete coverage would yield 36 within-window monthly percentage changes.

Monthly change = 100 × (current monthly price / previous calendar month price − 1). Stock prices use the last available adjusted closing price; gasoline prices use the mean of the dated weekly observations. The first selected month’s change is omitted. A missing previous calendar month makes that month’s stock return unavailable.

Selected-period totals require both selected endpoint prices: 100 × (last price / first price − 1). If a stock endpoint is missing, its selected-period total remains unavailable. An explicitly labeled overlap return is shown separately. Index charts start at 100 on the first month with both prices and retain gaps.

Pearson r is calculated only from months containing both changes. The trendline is ordinary least squares with an intercept. R² is the coefficient of determination, equal to r² for this regression. At least three valid pairs and nonzero variance in both variables are required. R² and r are different statistics.

Correlation describes contemporaneous association, not causation. The shorter available stock sample, October’s incomplete data, seasonality, broader markets, interest rates, and company events limit the interpretation. The dashboard does not test lagged effects or statistical significance.
